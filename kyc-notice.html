KYC & Identity Verification Privacy Notice

Notice Version: v1 Language: English (EN) Effective Date: November 2026

Summary: JemaBill Technologies Limited verifies customer identities using Bank Verification Number (BVN) or National Identification Number (NIN) alongside Date of Birth and biometric checks in compliance with Nigerian AML/CFT regulations and the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 (NDPA). This document details how your identity information is validated, processed, and safeguarded.

JemaBill Technologies Limited ("JemaBill", "we", "our", or "us") is committed to processing your personal data lawfully, fairly, and transparently. When you submit identity information to verify your account, you are providing consent for the processing described in this Notice.

1. Scope & Verification Tiers

We operate a tiered Know Your Customer (KYC) framework to protect user wallets and adhere to regulatory limits:

  • Tier 1 Verification: Validation of your legal full name, date of birth, and Nigerian phone number against your government identity number — either your 11-digit Bank Verification Number (BVN) or 11-digit National Identification Number (NIN).
  • Tier 2 Verification (Enhanced): In addition to Tier 1 details, Tier 2 requires an in-app interactive biometric challenge (real-time facial movement gestures) and a live selfie. Submissions undergo algorithmic liveness verification and manual compliance review before enhanced limits are granted.

2. Purpose of Collection & Legal Basis

Your identity data is processed strictly for the following legal and operational purposes:

  • Regulatory Compliance: Adhering to Central Bank of Nigeria (CBN) Anti-Money Laundering, Combating the Financing of Terrorism (AML/CFT), and Customer Due Diligence (CDD) directives.
  • Fraud Prevention & Identity Theft Protection: Ensuring that bank accounts, wallets, and bill payments are initiated by genuine, verified account owners.
  • Account Recovery & Security: Preventing unauthorized takeovers and verifying beneficial ownership during sensitive transaction queries.

Legal Basis: Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 (NDPA) and NDPR, our processing is based on your explicit consent, compliance with statutory legal obligations, and the performance of our contract with you.

3. Third-Party Identity Verification Partners

To verify your credentials, JemaBill securely queries official statutory databases (the Nigeria Inter-Bank Settlement System — NIBSS for BVN, and the National Identity Management Commission — NIMC for NIN) through licensed and audited identity partners:

  • Monnify (TeamApt Limited): Our primary identity verification provider. Monnify holds PCI-DSS Level 1 certification and NDPR data compliance licensing to transmit identity lookup requests directly to official regulatory clearing registries.

Our partners act solely as data processors under binding data processing agreements. They are legally prohibited from utilizing your identity data for any purpose other than executing the specific verification call requested.

4. Data Storage, Confidentiality & Encryption

We uphold strict cryptographic and architectural controls over identity records:

  • No Plaintext Storage of Sensitive Identifiers: We do not store raw, unencrypted BVN or NIN numbers in accessible plaintext databases. Values are securely transmitted via TLS 1.3 and hashed/encrypted at rest using industry-standard AES-256 encryption.
  • Biometric Data Protection: Facial challenge frames and selfie images submitted for Tier 2 verification are encrypted using dedicated 256-bit encryption keys. Access is strictly restricted to authorized compliance personnel during review audits.
  • Zero Data Selling: We never sell, lease, or monetize your identity information or KYC records to advertisers, marketers, or unauthorized brokers.

5. User Consent & Rights

By proceeding with identity verification on JemaBill, you confirm and agree that:

  • You are the legitimate holder of the submitted BVN or NIN and the associated date of birth.
  • You authorize JemaBill and its licensed identity verification partners to validate your submitted details against official government registers.
  • Your profile legal name will be synchronized and locked with the verified legal name returned by the registry to prevent account spoofing.

As a data subject under the NDPA 2023, you retain the right to request access to your verification status, request correction of inaccurate metadata, or lodge questions with our compliance department.

6. Retention Period

In accordance with Nigerian financial and anti-money laundering regulations, KYC verification audit trails and transaction records must be retained for a minimum statutory period of five (5) years following the closure of an account or termination of the business relationship.
